About Us

SKS Hardware is a trusted supplier of high-quality industrial valves, piping components, and hardware products.

We provide reliable industrial solutions for construction, water management, oil & gas, power plants, infrastructure projects, and general engineering industries.

With a commitment to quality, genuine products, and technical expertise, we deliver products from leading manufacturers to meet the diverse requirements of industrial applications. Our products comply with international standards — ISO 9001:2015, CE, API standards and PED.

Authorised distributor of L&T Valves · Authorised dealer of Pneutork · Authorised stockist of Zoloto Valves.
